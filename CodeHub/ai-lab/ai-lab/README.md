## Publishing

1. Open **Settings** in CodeHub.
2. Turn on **Publish as a website** and pick your subdomain.
3. Your site answers on `https://<subdomain>.htmltools.eu.cc`.

## Site icon

Add `favicon.ico` (or `favicon.png` / `icon.png`) to this folder — CodeHub serves
your own icon. Nothing is added for you, so a site without one simply uses the
browser default.
